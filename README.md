## McDonald’s Corporation Financial Performance Analysis (2002–2022)

This repository contains the financial performance analysis of McDonald’s Corporation over a 21-year period (2002–2022). The analysis was carried out using Excel and focuses on tracking key financial metrics, understanding long-term trends, and highlighting insights useful for management and investors.

---

### 📌 Objectives

- Track the long-term financial performance of McDonald’s.
- Identify periods of strong and weak performance.
- Explain major changes in profitability, returns, and capital structure.
- Visualize the company’s evolution over time using an Excel dashboard.

---

### 📊 Key Highlights

- **Revenue and earnings** showed consistent growth in the early years, with a sharp jump in earnings post-2015.
- **Profit margins** improved significantly, especially the operating margin which rose from 18% in 2002 to 43% in 2022.
- **ROE** and **ROA** increased after 2015, driven mostly by margin improvements and higher leverage.
- The **payout ratio** rose steadily, showing that dividends grew faster than earnings.
- Despite rising debt, **interest coverage remained strong**, and **net income** stayed positive.
- A breakdown using the **DuPont Model** shows that ROE gains were mainly from improved margins, not asset efficiency.
