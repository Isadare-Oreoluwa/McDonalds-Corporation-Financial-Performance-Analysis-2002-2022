# McDonald’s Corporation Financial Performance Analysis (2002–2022)

This repository contains a financial performance analysis of McDonald’s Corporation over a 21-year period (2002–2022). The analysis was carried out using Microsoft Excel and focuses on tracking key financial metrics, understanding long-term trends, and highlighting insights valuable to managers, investors, and financial analysts.

---

## 📌 Objectives

- Track the long-term financial performance of McDonald’s.
- Identify periods of strong and weak financial performance.
- Explain major changes in profitability, returns, and capital structure.
- Visualize the company's financial evolution using an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights

- **Revenue and earnings** showed consistent growth in the early years, with a sharp increase in earnings after 2015.
- **Profit margins** improved significantly, with operating margin rising from **18% in 2002** to **43% in 2022**.
- **ROE** and **ROA** increased after 2015, driven primarily by improved profitability and increased financial leverage.
- The **dividend payout ratio** rose steadily, indicating dividends grew faster than earnings.
- Despite increasing debt levels, **interest coverage remained strong**, while **net income** stayed consistently positive.
- **DuPont Analysis** reveals that improvements in ROE were driven mainly by higher profit margins rather than increased asset efficiency.
